Privacy Policy

The tool uses Google authorization to read selected Gmail messages and attachments, create and access files through its own Google Drive app access, and send inventory and operational emails. Gmail read authorization technically covers the owner's mailbox; the implementation selects evidence using configured labels, sender allowlists, date ranges and statement-search rules.

Selected original emails, attachments and integrity manifests are stored as ordinary, unencrypted ZIP files in a private Google Drive folder owned by the authorizing account. Google-protected/password-protected source documents retain their original protection. Anyone who obtains access to the saved ZIP files can read their unprotected contents.

Scheduled processing runs through a private GitHub repository's GitHub Actions workflow. Google authorization credentials are kept as private Actions secrets. Documents are processed in the runner's memory and are not intentionally placed in repository commits, workflow artifacts, caches or logs. The tool relies on Google and GitHub infrastructure to provide these functions.

The tool does not sell user data, use it for advertising, train AI models on it, or share financial documents with unrelated third parties. Inventory emails contain aggregate counts and operational status; they do not attach financial documents.

The account owner controls retention and deletion through Google Drive and Gmail. The bot does not automatically delete existing evidence. Access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account connection settings, and scheduled operation can be disabled in the private repository.

The tool is for personal use only. Support and developer contact details are supplied to the owner through the Google consent configuration. These public pages do not collect visitor form submissions or operate a public document-upload service.
