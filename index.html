Financial Evidence Backup Bot

Financial Evidence Backup Bot is a personal-use tool that preserves selected financial statement emails and document attachments in the account owner's Google Drive. It creates SHA-256 integrity manifests and sends inventory and operational-alert emails to the same account owner. It is not a banking, trading, payment or investment-advice service.

The tool is operated for its owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public service and is not intended for third-party account access.
